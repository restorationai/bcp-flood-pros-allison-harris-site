## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process

We start with an assessment of what materials are affected, carpet, padding, subfloor, drywall, upholstery, before deciding what can be cleaned and what needs to be removed. Affected areas are contained to stop cross-contamination into hallways or HVAC returns. Surfaces are cleaned and disinfected following the same bloodborne pathogen handling practices used across the industry, then the space is deodorized, since biological odor tends to linger in porous materials long after visible traces are gone. Any regulated waste is bagged, labeled, and transported according to state-regulated medical waste guidelines, not placed in standard household trash. We do a final walkthrough with the homeowner or property manager before we consider the job closed.
