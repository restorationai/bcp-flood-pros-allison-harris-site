## Local note

One thing that trips up out-of-town contractors working in Springville: older brick homes near downtown were often built with a lime-based mortar rather than modern Portland cement mortar, and repointing or patching brick with the wrong mix can trap moisture behind the wall and cause spalling within a year or two. We test or ask about mortar type before doing any masonry patch work on pre-war homes, which saves a second trip and a second claim down the road.
