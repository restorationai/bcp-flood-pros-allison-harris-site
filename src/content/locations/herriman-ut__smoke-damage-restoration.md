## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Herriman

We start with an assessment of how far the smoke traveled, checking HVAC ducting, attic space, and wall cavities, not just the visibly affected room. Dry soot gets removed with HEPA vacuuming and dry sponges before any wet cleaning touches a surface, since introducing moisture to dry soot too early can drive it deeper into porous materials like drywall and grout. From there we move to odor source removal: sealing or replacing affected insulation, cleaning or replacing HVAC filters and ductwork runs, and treating hard surfaces with cleaners matched to the residue type (protein, synthetic, or wildfire ash each respond differently). Thermal fogging or ozone treatment, applied per industry drying and deodorization standards, follows once the source material has been addressed, because masking odor over an unresolved source just means it comes back.
