## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Provo

Every job starts with an assessment of what materials are affected and whether they can be cleaned or need to be removed entirely. Framing, drywall, carpet padding, and subflooring that have absorbed biological material typically can't be saved and are removed following OSHA bloodborne pathogen handling guidelines. We contain the work area before anything is disturbed, using barriers and negative air where needed so the rest of the home or unit isn't affected. Regulated waste is packaged and transported according to Utah's medical waste handling rules, never placed in standard household trash. Once materials are removed, we clean and disinfect remaining surfaces, then run odor neutralization equipment, since Provo's dry air means residual odor can linger in porous materials longer than homeowners expect if it isn't treated directly at the source.
