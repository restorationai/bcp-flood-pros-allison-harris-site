**Rebuilding after fire, water, or storm damage in Lehi?** Reconstruction Services from FIX Restoration takes a property from gutted and exposed back to livable, matching the fast-growing building stock that defines this part of Utah County. Whether it's a newer build off the Point of the Mountain corridor or an older farmhouse near the original town center, the rebuild has to account for how differently these structures were framed, insulated, and permitted.

## Why Lehi Properties Need Specialized Reconstruction

Lehi has grown faster than almost any city in Utah County over the past two decades, and that growth shows up in the mix of construction we see on reconstruction jobs. Newer subdivisions built during the Silicon Slopes expansion use engineered trusses, OSB sheathing, and open-concept framing that behaves predictably once water or fire damage is cut out, but the handful of older homes closer to Lehi's original grid often have balloon framing or full dimensional lumber that changes how a wall assembly needs to be rebuilt to pass inspection. Clay-heavy soils common throughout the valley also mean foundations and slab-on-grade additions can shift slightly after a major water event, which is something we check before closing up drywall.

## Our Reconstruction Process in Lehi

Reconstruction starts the moment demolition and drying are complete, not after. We document every removed stud, header, and section of subfloor with photos tied to the original loss so the rebuild scope lines up with what the adjuster and homeowner already agreed to. Framing and subfloor repairs are brought back to match existing structural members, moisture levels are verified with a meter before any new drywall or flooring goes down, and finish work, paint, trim, and flooring, is matched as closely as possible to the home's existing materials. For fire damage reconstruction specifically, we coordinate smoke sealing and odor treatment before framing closes back up, since trapped smoke behind new drywall is one of the most common callbacks in this trade.

## Lehi Permitting and HOA Coordination

Building permits for structural reconstruction in Lehi are issued through the city, and any work touching load-bearing framing, electrical, or plumbing typically needs inspection sign-off before drywall goes back up. A large share of Lehi's newer neighborhoods are governed by homeowners associations with their own exterior material and color requirements, which matters most on fire or storm jobs where siding, roofing, or exterior trim has to be replaced. We pull the required permits and loop in HOA architectural review early so a rebuild doesn't stall waiting on approval for something as small as a shingle color match.

## Local note

One detail that catches first-time rebuild clients off guard in Lehi: because so much of the city was built out within the last fifteen years, matching replacement siding, trim profiles, or roofing shingles to an existing newer home can actually be harder than matching materials on an older house, since some original builder-grade products are no longer stocked by local suppliers. We source close matches early in the estimate phase rather than waiting until framing is done, so the homeowner isn't surprised by a visible seam between old and new exterior material.
