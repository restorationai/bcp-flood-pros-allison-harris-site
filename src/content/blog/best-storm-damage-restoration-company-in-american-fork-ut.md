## Will the company document the damage the way your insurance claim needs?

A storm damage company should arrive with a camera and a moisture meter, not just a tarp, because your insurance adjuster will ask for dated photos, moisture logs, and a written scope before approving payment. Most homeowners' policies cover sudden wind and hail damage, but the claim moves faster and cleaner when the mitigation company captures the evidence as the work happens, not after the fact.

Ask any company you're considering these direct questions before you sign anything:

- Will you photograph the damage before tarping or removing anything?
- Do you provide a written scope of work, not a verbal estimate?
- Will you keep a daily moisture log if there's interior water intrusion?
- Can your documentation go directly to my adjuster, or do I have to relay it?
- Is the estimate itemized by task, or one lump sum?

A company that hesitates on any of these is telling you something. Good documentation protects you if the adjuster pushes back on scope, and it's standard practice for a company that works insurance claims regularly, per the [Insurance Information Institute's guidance on settling claims after a disaster](https://www.iii.org/article/settling-insurance-claims-after-a-disaster).
